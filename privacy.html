Privacy Policy for Quick Search KJV Bible

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Overview

The Quick Search KJV Bible application is built as a free, offline utility. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as-is.

Information Collection and Use

Quick Search KJV Bible is a fully static, offline Bible application:

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any personal information, location data, or device identifiers.
  • No Third-Party Services: The application does not integrate third-party SDKs, tracking frameworks, analytics services, or advertising networks.
  • Local Device Storage: Any bookmarks, search histories, notes, or highlights you create stay exclusively on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

Device Permissions

The app operates locally and does not request access to sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever from any user, it does not collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page with an updated revision date.
